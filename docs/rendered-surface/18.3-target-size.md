# 18.3 — A floor under the targets

Spec: [`../rendered-surface.md`](../rendered-surface.md), decision 4. Status:
[`../refactor-plan.md`](../refactor-plan.md).

**Goal.** Turn rule 3 green. "Đổi" measures 20 × 32 against a WCAG 2.2 AA floor
of 24 × 24.

## Files

- `assets/css/omniwp.css`

## Steps

1. `.sl-action` gains `min-width`, matching the `min-height` 17.3 gave it. A
   floor on the class, not padding on the instance: padding makes the number
   depend on the word, and "Đổi" is the shortest label the card has *today*, not
   the shortest it can have.
2. 24px, not 44. These are text controls in a dense list of rows; a 44px floor
   costs more row height than the control gains.
3. Check the row does not grow: `.sl-row` has `min-height: 36px` and the control
   is 32, so there is room — but that is a prediction, and 17.2 is what this
   phase learned about predictions.

## Acceptance

- Rule 3 green
- **Measured, with the numbers written down**: every `.sl-action` in a rendered
  account card returns a bounding box of at least 24 × 24, and `.sl-row` returns
  the same height it did before
- Every other suite at its previous count

## Not in scope

The rest of the plugin's controls. `.sl-btn` is well past the floor on its own
padding and `.sl-toggle-password` is 32 × 32 by declaration; both were checked
while writing this and neither needs changing.

Measured either side of the change, in a browser, against
`build/visual/account.html`:

| | Đổi | Bỏ liên kết | Liên kết Zalo | `.sl-row` heights | Contact card |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Before | **20 × 32** | 60 × 32 | 45 × 32 | 36, 36, 36, 36 | 304px |
| After | **24 × 32** | 60 × 32 | 45 × 32 | 36, 36, 36, 36 | 304px |

Controls under the floor: 2 → 0. Unchanged at 375, 480 and 1400 — the floor is
on the class, so it does not depend on how much room the row has.

### `justify-content: center` went with it

A 24px box holding a 20px word puts the text against its left edge without it,
which is visible the moment the floor starts doing anything. One line, and it
only matters for the controls the floor is now widening.

### The prediction was right, and it is now a reading

The brief predicted the row would not grow, from `.sl-row`'s `min-height: 36px`
against a 32px control. It did not. But 17.2 made the same kind of prediction
from the same kind of arithmetic and was wrong in both magnitude and direction,
which is why this one was measured rather than argued.

Suite fully green and promoted to `required` here, in the sub-phase that turned
the last rule.
