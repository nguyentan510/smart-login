## Two findings, and neither is fixed here

The brief says a measurement that finds something becomes the next phase's
material. Conflating the two is how a measurement stops being trustworthy.

### A. The plugin declares a focus style for its inputs and not for its controls

Every `:focus` rule in the stylesheet:

```text
.sl-input:focus
.sl-input[aria-invalid="true"]:focus
.sl-otp-digit:focus
.sl-action--danger:hover, .sl-action--danger:focus-visible
```

`.sl-btn` and plain `.sl-action` have none, and fall back to the browser's ring —
which is visible, and which a theme carrying `*:focus { outline: none }` removes.
That is the same shape as 17.3's `.screen-reader-text`: the plugin already
declined to trust themes for one property and did not for its neighbour.

### B. With JavaScript off, "Đổi" is a dead control with no explanation

The contact editor is `hidden` and opened by a listener. With no JavaScript, the
button does nothing and says nothing. `partials/address-fields.php:91-95` has a
`<noscript>` doing exactly this job for the ward select; the contact card has
none.

Found because the renderer produces a page with no JavaScript at all — the
reading this tool gives away for free, and the one nobody had taken in ten
phases.
