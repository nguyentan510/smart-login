# 9.8 — Adaptive captcha

Spec: [`../abuse-boundary.md`](../abuse-boundary.md). Status:
[`../refactor-plan.md`](../refactor-plan.md).

**Blocked on [9.1](9.1-site-budget.md) and [9.3](9.3-delivery-limits.md).**
Adaptive mode reads budget pressure and breaker state, so both have to exist and
be measurable. And verification is another blocking outbound call in the request
path — it must inherit 9.3's timeout discipline or it reintroduces the exact bug
9.3 fixes.

**Goal.** A challenge that appears when the site is under pressure and is
invisible the rest of the time.

## Files

- `includes/Security/class-captcha.php` — new, provider behind a small interface
- `includes/Frontend/class-form-controller.php`
- `includes/Frontend/class-rest-controller.php`
- `includes/class-field-registry.php` — `security` tab, `captcha` section
- `templates/partials/`, `assets/js/`
- `includes/Admin/class-readiness.php`

## Steps

1. **Provider interface**, so adding hCaptcha later is a class rather than a
   rewrite. Settings: `provider` (`off|turnstile|hcaptcha`), `site_key`,
   `secret`, `mode`.

2. **The secret is encrypted on save and never re-rendered**, exactly as the
   OAuth provider secrets already are. Reuse that path; do not invent a second
   one.

3. **`mode` is the design decision.** `always` challenges everyone. `adaptive` —
   the default — challenges only when the request is already suspicious:
   - the site budget is over a configurable fraction (default 50%) of its hourly
     ceiling, **or**
   - the kill switch tripped within the last hour, **or**
   - the circuit breaker is open, **or**
   - this IP has spent more than half of `max_identify_per_ip_hour`

   Ordinary visitors on an ordinary day see nothing. The challenge appears when
   the cost of an extra click is worth paying, and not before.

4. **Enforce at every entry that can cause an SMS:** `handle_identify`,
   `handle_forgot`, and REST `/register` and `/forgot`.

5. **Timeout ≤ 5 s, hard-clamped.** A captcha endpoint that hangs must not become
   the worker-exhaustion bug a second time.

6. **Fail closed.** Verification that cannot complete refuses the send. The spec
   explains why this points the opposite way to the `Client::ip()` decision, and
   why both are right.

## Acceptance

With `mode = adaptive` and the budget under pressure, the identify form renders a
widget and rejects a submission carrying no valid token. Under no pressure the
widget is **absent** and submissions pass untouched — the second half is the one
that decides whether this is shippable, since a challenge that shows up on a
quiet Tuesday is a conversion bug.

A stubbed verifier that times out refuses the send.

## Not in scope

Bot scoring, device fingerprinting, or anything resembling a WAF. The spec's
ownership table puts that with the CDN.
