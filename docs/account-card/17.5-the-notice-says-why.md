# 17.5 — The notice says why

Spec: [`../account-card.md`](../account-card.md), decision 5. Status:
[`../refactor-plan.md`](../refactor-plan.md).

**Goal.** Turn rule 6 green. The box currently reads "Địa chỉ, Ngày sinh". The
sentence that makes each of those worth filling in is already written, already
translated, and read by one screen only.

## Files

- `templates/partials/account/status.php`
- `assets/css/omniwp.css` — the notice list

## Steps

1. `onboarding_reasons()` becomes public API for this partial too — it already
   is `public static`; the change is that a second caller exists.
2. The notice renders one line per missing item: label, then its reason, then the
   existing action link. Items with no reason render the label alone — `email`
   deliberately has none, and inventing one here would be the copy drifting from
   the place it lives.
3. No reason string is written into the template.

## Acceptance

- Rule 6 green, both halves — the reason renders, and it is not a literal in the
  template
- An item with no reason still renders
- Every other suite at its previous count

## Not in scope

The onboarding screen, which already reads these strings and is unchanged.

The box rendered `implode( ', ', … labels )` — on a live page, a coloured panel
containing the words "Địa chỉ nhận hàng, Ngày sinh". It now reads:

```text
Địa chỉ nhận hàng   Để đơn hàng được giao đúng nơi, không phải nhập lại mỗi lần
Ngày sinh           Để nhận ưu đãi vào dịp sinh nhật
```

Looked up, never copied — the rule has two halves for that reason, and the
second one (the string is not a literal in the template) passed vacuously in
17.0 and means something now.

### The two branches turned out to be one

They differed in a CSS modifier, a heading and the wording of a link, and
carried a duplicated list, a duplicated action link and a duplicated conditional
between them. Choosing the set first and rendering once removed all three copies
— not in the brief, and the change the brief's step 2 turned out to require.

Required still outranks recommended, which is the behaviour that was there:
somebody with both is shown the half they cannot proceed without.
