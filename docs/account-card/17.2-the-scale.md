# 17.2 — The scale

Spec: [`../account-card.md`](../account-card.md), decision 1. Status:
[`../refactor-plan.md`](../refactor-plan.md).

**Goal.** Turn rule 2 green. Six spacing steps and five type steps, declared
once, read by the account surface.

Sequenced before 17.3 and 17.8 because both write new CSS. Writing it in
literals and converting afterwards is two edits to the same lines.

## Files

- `assets/css/omniwp.css`

## Steps

1. Declare the tokens on `.omniwp`, beside the colours that are already
   there: `--sl-space-1..6` (4/8/12/16/20/24), `--sl-fs-xs..xl`
   (12/13/15/16/20), `--sl-radius-card`, `--sl-surface-2`.
2. Convert the account-surface region and the shared components the card draws
   from (`.sl-field`, `.sl-label`, `.sl-input`, `.sl-btn`, `.sl-badge`,
   `.sl-hint`) to read them.
3. Move the five off-scale values named in the spec: card padding `18/20` →
   `16/20`, row gap `10` → `12`, badge `11px` → `12px`, `.sl-static` padding
   `9` → `8`, `.sl-btn` padding `13` → `12`.
4. `.sl-btn` gains `line-height: 1.4`, so it computes to the input's 47px by
   construction rather than by luck.
5. Delete both negative margins. `.sl-card__note` stops needing `-8px` because
   the title's bottom margin is what it was cancelling; give the title no bottom
   margin when a note follows, using the adjacent sibling rather than
   subtraction.

### The measurement corrected the finding it was meant to confirm

Finding 2 in the spec predicted the button inherits `line-height: 1.5` from
`.omniwp` and comes out near 50px against the input's 47. Measured in a
browser against this stylesheet, with `.sl-btn` reverted to its previous values:

| | input | button | button line-height | button padding |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Before | 47px | **45px** | `normal` | 13 / 13 |
| After | 46.6px | 46.6px | 21px | 12 / 12 |

A `button` gets `line-height: normal` from the UA stylesheet, and that beats
inheritance. So the button was the *shorter* of the two by 2px, not the taller
by 3 — right defect, wrong number, wrong direction. The spec was corrected
rather than left describing something that does not happen.

This is the one thing here a test cannot see, which is why the brief made it a
completion condition. It is also the second time in two phases that reading the
file produced a confident wrong answer about it: 16.3's width reading had the
same shape.

### Both negative margins went, and neither was replaced by a smaller one

`.sl-card__note`'s `-8px` was cancelling the theme's default paragraph margin
*plus* part of the title's — a distance nothing in this file controls, arrived
at by trying numbers. `.sl-hint--reason`'s `-3px` was cancelling part of the
label's. Both state their own margins now.

### Not done here, and why

The stylesheet outside the account region keeps its literals — `.sl-input`'s
14px horizontal padding, `.sl-label`'s 6px, `.sl-input--lg`'s 15px. Forcing
those onto six steps changes the sign-in screens Phase 16 has just finished
measuring, in a commit about the account card. The deferral is in the spec and
in the token block itself.
